Overview

This policy explains how we handle information through the forms and contact methods available on this website. This website is a sample (demo) site and contains sample content for products and services, which is not a real business commitment.

What we collect

  • Name, phone, email, location and message that you provide in the form.
  • The language and interest area you select.
  • Small technical preferences such as language choice, stored only in your browser.

How information is used

  • To answer your enquiry and stay in contact with you.
  • To show related content in the language you chose.

Sharing

When you submit the form, the message opens directly in WhatsApp; its delivery is handled according to WhatsApp and its technology providers' policies. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Storage

Form data is not stored in our database. Preferences such as language are kept only in your browser's local storage.

Your choices

You can contact us through the contact page to request deletion or correction of information you have shared.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please reach us through the contact page.
